## Gameplay Features and Systems

### Dylan Faden and the Central Story

CONTROL Resonant continues the narrative of Remedy Entertainment's CONTROL universe, shifting the main playable role from Jesse Faden to her brother Dylan.

After years of confinement and a difficult history with the Federal Bureau of Control, Dylan awakens during a new paranatural crisis. Manhattan has been transformed by an extraordinary force, and familiar surroundings no longer follow predictable rules.

Dylan must navigate this altered environment, confront dangerous entities, and search for Jesse. His journey also explores his identity, his relationship with the Bureau, and the consequences of events that shaped his past.

The story is designed to be accessible to newcomers while offering returning players additional context about the Faden siblings and the mysteries surrounding the CONTROL universe.

### A Distorted Manhattan

The game moves beyond the confined corridors of the Oldest House and into a transformed Manhattan. Instead of presenting the city as an ordinary urban environment, it reshapes familiar spaces through paranatural phenomena.

Buildings can appear to fold into impossible arrangements, streets can become unfamiliar routes, and gravity can behave in unexpected ways. These changes influence both the visual presentation and the way players navigate each location.

The city is organized into distinct, expansive zones rather than one uninterrupted map. Each area contains its own encounters, activities, and discoveries, giving exploration a recognizable structure while preserving a sense of uncertainty.

### The Aberrant Shape-Shifting Weapon

Dylan's signature weapon, the Aberrant, is a central part of the combat system. It changes form to support different melee approaches, allowing players to adapt their attacks to enemies and battlefield conditions.

Revealed forms include the Hammer, Blades, Scythe, and Fists. Each form supports a different style of engagement, from powerful strikes to faster combinations and more flexible attack sequences.

The weapon is connected to character development. As players advance, additional forms and combat options become available, creating opportunities to refine a preferred approach rather than relying on a single attack pattern.

### Supernatural Abilities and Movement

Dylan's abilities affect both combat and traversal. They help him move through locations where ordinary movement rules are unreliable and provide additional options when navigating distorted architecture.

Reach supports movement across difficult spaces, while Shift helps Dylan adapt to altered gravity and changing orientation. These abilities are particularly important in areas where walls, roads, and other surfaces no longer behave as expected.

Movement is therefore part of the game's broader problem-solving experience. Players must observe the environment, identify viable routes, and learn how supernatural abilities interact with the spaces around them.

### Gravity Anomalies and Environmental Navigation

Gravity Anomalies create sections where direction and orientation can change. A surface that would normally be a ceiling or wall may become a traversable route, requiring players to reconsider their understanding of the surrounding architecture.

These areas combine environmental observation with the use of Dylan's movement abilities. Successful navigation depends on recognizing accessible surfaces, adjusting to changes in orientation, and finding a route through an altered space.

The same reality-bending design contributes to the game's atmosphere. Environments communicate that the crisis is changing the underlying rules of the world, not merely introducing new enemies into familiar locations.

### Combat Progression and Customization

CONTROL Resonant offers a development system built around supernatural abilities, weapon forms, combat combinations, and specialized upgrades.

Players can unlock additional Aberrant forms, develop abilities, and customize their approach to encounters. Different combinations allow a focus on heavy attacks, rapid melee sequences, movement, or other available combat options.

Loadout choices are important when preparing for difficult encounters. Reviewing available abilities and selecting complementary weapon forms can help players develop a consistent combat rhythm while leaving room to adapt as new threats appear.

### Resonants and Major Boss Encounters

Resonants are powerful enemies connected to the mysterious force threatening reality. These encounters are central to the game's combat and character development systems.

Defeating Resonants provides new combat abilities, linking boss encounters to Dylan's growing capabilities. Progress therefore comes not only from overcoming a difficult opponent but also from expanding the options available in later encounters.

Boss battles encourage players to observe enemy behaviour, manage their positioning, and make effective use of their weapon forms and supernatural abilities. Each major confrontation serves as a test of the tools developed throughout the journey.

### Exploration, Side Activities, and Secrets

The game's distinct zones contain more than the central story missions. Players can discover optional encounters, hidden locations, side activities, and environmental details that add context to the transformed city.

Exploration rewards curiosity. Investigating an unusual passage or looking beyond the most direct route may reveal additional encounters or useful discoveries.

The zone-based structure gives each location its own identity while maintaining a clear connection to the central narrative. Players can balance story advancement with optional exploration according to their preferred pace.

### Supporting Characters and Field Guidance

Dylan's journey includes interactions with other characters involved in the response to the paranatural crisis. Zoe De Vera, an FBC field agent, acts as his handler and provides guidance as he navigates unfamiliar territory.

These interactions supply context for new locations, explain aspects of the unfolding emergency, and contribute to Dylan's personal development. They also help connect exploration and combat with the larger events affecting Manhattan.

Jesse remains important to the story even though Dylan is the main playable character. The siblings' connected histories provide a continuing thread between the original CONTROL and this next chapter.

### Atmosphere, Sound, and Visual Design

CONTROL Resonant combines science-fiction imagery, supernatural phenomena, and environmental storytelling to create a distinctive atmosphere.

The altered cityscape, shifting geometry, unusual lighting, and enemy designs reinforce the feeling that reality itself is under pressure. Rather than relying exclusively on dialogue, the environments communicate the scale and consequences of the crisis through what players encounter while exploring.

The soundtrack also supports the emotional direction of the story. Music, environmental audio, combat effects, and changes in intensity help distinguish quieter exploration from more demanding encounters.
